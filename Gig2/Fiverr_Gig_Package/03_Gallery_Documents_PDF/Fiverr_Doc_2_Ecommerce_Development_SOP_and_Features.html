2. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) UX Architecture

Persistent LocalStorage Cart

Shoppers never lose their selections. Cart state, quantities, and selected variants are automatically persisted client-side across page refreshes and browser tabs.

Frictionless Slide-out Drawer

Customers can view items, adjust quantities, calculate free-shipping thresholds, and apply promotional discounts without ever leaving their browsing view.

Instant Search & Multi-Filters

Zero-latency client-side search and category filtering enable buyers to pinpoint products in milliseconds, directly boosting checkout conversions.
