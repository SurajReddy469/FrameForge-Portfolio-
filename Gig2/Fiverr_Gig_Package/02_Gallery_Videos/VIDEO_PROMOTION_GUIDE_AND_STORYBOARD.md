# 🎬 Fiverr E-Commerce Video Promo Guide & Storyboard

**Gig Service:** Luxury & High-Converting E-Commerce Websites (React & Tailwind CSS)  
**Target Video Duration:** 15 – 30 seconds (or 8-second Veo cinematic reel)  
**Recommended Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) Widescreen  

---

## 📌 Fiverr Video Policy Rules to Keep in Mind
1. **Fiverr Exclusivity Outro:** Fiverr strongly favors videos that display *"Exclusively on Fiverr"* or *"Available on Fiverr"* in the final 2–3 seconds.
2. **First 3 Seconds Hook:** Show high-contrast movement (e.g. rotating 3D luxury watch, sliding cart drawer, optical loupe zoom) within 3 seconds to keep buyers from scrolling past.
3. **Keep Music Non-Copyright / Ambient:** Use high-end lo-fi chillhop or modern ambient synth audio.
